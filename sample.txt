AgentX Hackathon Team Notes

Our team has three members. The team lead builds the AI engine in Python.
Teammate 2 builds the React frontend with Vite and Tailwind, deployed on Vercel.
Teammate 3 builds the FastAPI backend with SQLite for history, deployed on Render.

The hackathon takes place on 24-25 July 2026. The problem statement is revealed
at 9:40 AM on the first day. Our goal is to adapt the skeleton to any domain in
under 2 hours.

The AI stack uses a provider-agnostic LLM wrapper with Gemini as the primary
provider. RAG is handled by ChromaDB with local sentence-transformers embeddings,
so document embedding costs nothing.

Checkpoint 1 is Sunday 19 July: engine.py working locally.
Checkpoint 2 is Tuesday 21 July: full integration deployed.
Checkpoint 3 is Wednesday 22 July: timed dry-run with a mock problem.
