# Hostel Rules and Regulations

Document Number: VCE/HOS/RULES/2024
Effective Date: 01 June 2024
Approved by: Hostel Advisory Committee

## 1. Admission to Hostel

1.1 Admission to the hostel shall be for one academic year and shall be
renewed annually. Continued residence shall be subject to satisfactory
conduct and clearance of all dues.

1.2 Allotment of rooms shall be made by the Chief Warden. No candidate shall
change rooms without prior written permission.

## 2. Conduct and Discipline

2.1 Residents shall maintain silence in the hostel premises between 22:00
hours and 06:00 hours.

2.2 The possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, narcotics, or any
prohibited substance within the hostel premises is strictly forbidden and
shall attract immediate expulsion from the hostel.

2.3 Ragging in any form is prohibited and shall be dealt with under the
Anti-Ragging Regulations and the applicable directions of the Supreme Court
and the All India Council for Technical Education.

2.4 Residents shall not keep electrical appliances such as heaters, induction
stoves, or immersion rods in their rooms. Such appliances found shall be
confiscated and a fine levied.

## 3. Leave and Absence

3.1 A resident proceeding on leave shall record the same in the movement
register maintained by the Warden and shall obtain the prior approval of the
Warden for absence exceeding twenty-four hours.

3.2 A resident shall obtain the written consent of the parent or guardian for
any absence extending beyond a weekend.

## 4. No-Dues and Vacation

4.1 A resident vacating the hostel shall obtain a no-dues certificate from
the Warden, the Mess Secretary, and the Accounts Office.

4.2 The no-dues certificate shall be a pre-condition for the release of the
caution deposit and for registration for the succeeding semester under
Academic Regulations R22 clause 3.3.

4.3 Rooms shall be vacated within seven days of the last examination of the
semester unless prior permission for extended stay has been granted.
