# Library Rules

Document Number: VCE/LIB/RULES/2024
Effective Date: 01 July 2024
Approved by: Library Advisory Committee

## 1. Membership and Borrowing

1.1 Every candidate on the rolls of the College shall be a member of the
Central Library upon registration for the semester.

1.2 An undergraduate candidate may borrow up to four volumes at any one time.
A postgraduate candidate may borrow up to six volumes.

1.3 The loan period for a general collection volume shall be fourteen days.
Reference volumes, journals, and rare materials shall not be issued for home
use.

## 2. Renewal and Overdue Charges

2.1 A volume may be renewed twice, provided it has not been reserved by
another member. Renewal shall be effected before the due date.

2.2 An overdue charge of rupees two (Rs. 2) per volume per day shall be
levied for volumes retained beyond the due date.

2.3 A member with overdue volumes or unpaid overdue charges shall not be
issued further volumes until the same are returned and cleared.

2.4 Library dues outstanding at the end of a semester shall be treated as
dues payable to the College for the purposes of Academic Regulations R22
clause 3.3.

## 3. Loss and Damage

3.1 A member losing a borrowed volume shall replace it with a copy of the
same or a later edition, or pay twice the current cost of the volume as
assessed by the Librarian.

3.2 Marking, underlining, or removing pages from library material shall
attract a penalty and may result in suspension of borrowing privileges.

## 4. Electronic Resources

4.1 Access to subscribed electronic resources shall be through the
institutional network and the credentials issued to each member.

4.2 Systematic or bulk downloading of content from subscribed electronic
resources is prohibited and may result in suspension of institutional access
by the publisher.
