# Student Grievance Redressal Standard Operating Procedure

Document Number: VCE/SGRC/SOP/2024
Effective Date: 01 August 2024
Approved by: Student Grievance Redressal Committee

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This procedure establishes the mechanism for redressal of grievances of
candidates relating to academic matters, examinations, hostel and mess
facilities, library services, fees, and conduct of staff.

1.2 Matters relating to ragging shall be dealt with exclusively under the
Anti-Ragging Regulations and shall not be routed through this procedure.

## 2. Filing a Grievance

2.1 A grievance shall be filed in writing, or through the student portal, and
shall state the facts, the relief sought, and the particulars of the
candidate.

2.2 An anonymous grievance shall not ordinarily be entertained, save where
the Committee is satisfied that the matter discloses a serious risk to the
safety or welfare of any person.

## 3. Escalation Ladder and Service Levels

3.1 Level 1 — The grievance shall first be placed before the Class Counsellor
or the Warden, as appropriate, who shall dispose of it within three working
days.

3.2 Level 2 — Where the grievance is not resolved at Level 1, or the
candidate is dissatisfied with the outcome, it shall be escalated to the Head
of the Department concerned, who shall dispose of it within seven working
days.

3.3 Level 3 — Where the grievance remains unresolved, it shall be placed
before the Student Grievance Redressal Committee, which shall dispose of it
within fifteen working days.

3.4 Level 4 — A candidate aggrieved by the decision of the Committee may
prefer an appeal to the Principal within ten days of the communication of the
decision. The decision of the Principal shall be final.

## 4. Records and Confidentiality

4.1 A register of grievances shall be maintained recording the date of
receipt, the nature of the grievance, the level at which it was disposed of,
and the date of disposal.

4.2 The identity of a complainant shall not be disclosed except to the extent
necessary for the disposal of the grievance.

4.3 No candidate shall be subjected to any disadvantage by reason of having
filed a grievance in good faith.
