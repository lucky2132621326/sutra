# Placement and Training Policy

Document Number: VCE/TPO/POL/2025
Effective Date: 15 June 2025
Approved by: Training and Placement Committee

## 1. Preamble

1.1 This policy governs the conduct of campus recruitment, pre-placement
training, and internship placement for all candidates of the College.

1.2 The Training and Placement Office (TPO) shall be the sole authority for
coordinating campus recruitment activity. No candidate shall correspond
directly with a visiting recruiter except through the TPO.

## 2. Eligibility for Campus Recruitment

2.1 A candidate shall be eligible to participate in campus recruitment only
if the candidate has registered with the Training and Placement Office and
has paid the prescribed registration fee for the academic year.

2.2 The minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) required for
participation shall be as stipulated by the visiting organisation. Where no
such stipulation is made, the College shall apply a minimum CGPA of 6.0.

2.3 A candidate having any active backlog at the time of the recruitment
drive shall not be eligible to participate, unless the visiting organisation
expressly permits candidates with backlogs.

2.4 Eligibility shall be determined with reference to the branch, batch year,
CGPA, and backlog position of the candidate as recorded in the official
records of the Office of the Controller of Examinations as on the date of the
drive. Records subsequently revised shall not confer retrospective
eligibility.

2.5 A candidate found to have furnished incorrect particulars regarding CGPA,
backlogs, or branch shall be debarred from all campus recruitment activity
for the remainder of the programme.

## 3. The One-Offer Rule

3.1 A candidate who has received and accepted an offer of employment through
campus recruitment shall not be permitted to participate in any subsequent
recruitment drive, save as provided in clause 3.2.

3.2 A candidate holding an offer may be permitted to appear for a
"dream company" drive, being an organisation offering a compensation package
of not less than twice the package already secured, on one occasion only, and
with the prior written permission of the Training and Placement Officer.

3.3 A candidate who declines an offer made through campus recruitment shall
be treated as placed for the purposes of clause 3.1.

## 4. Internship Placement

4.1 Internship recruitment shall ordinarily be conducted during the sixth
semester for candidates of the third year.

4.2 Eligibility criteria for internship recruitment shall be determined by
the visiting organisation and notified by the TPO not less than five working
days before the drive.

4.3 A candidate selected for an internship shall be required to complete the
internship in full. Withdrawal from a confirmed internship without the
written permission of the TPO shall render the candidate ineligible for
subsequent campus recruitment.

## 5. Mandatory Pre-Placement Training

5.1 All candidates registered for campus recruitment shall attend the
pre-placement training programme conducted by the TPO, comprising aptitude
training, technical training, and soft skills training.

5.2 A minimum attendance of eighty percent (80%) in the pre-placement
training programme shall be mandatory. A candidate failing to secure such
attendance may be declared ineligible for campus recruitment at the
discretion of the Training and Placement Officer.

5.3 Placement preparation workshops shall be scheduled so as not to conflict
with regular instruction. Where a conflict is unavoidable, attendance in the
regular instructional session shall take precedence, and the candidate shall
attend an alternate batch of the workshop where one is offered.

5.4 A candidate shall not be permitted to substitute attendance in a
placement workshop for attendance in a regular course of instruction, and no
attendance credit under Academic Regulations R22 clause 4.2 shall accrue by
reason of attendance at a placement activity.
