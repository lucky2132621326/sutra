# Academic Regulations R22

Document Number: VCE/ACAD/R22/2022
Effective Date: 01 July 2022
Approved by: Academic Council, Vasavi College of Engineering (Autonomous)

## 1. Scope and Applicability

1.1 These regulations shall apply to all candidates admitted to the four-year
B.E. degree programmes from the academic year 2022-23 onwards under the
autonomous scheme of the College.

1.2 The provisions herein supersede all previous regulations (R18, R20) for
the batches specified in clause 1.1. Candidates on earlier regulations shall
continue to be governed by the regulations in force at the time of their
admission.

1.3 The Academic Council reserves the right to revise these regulations. Any
revision shall be notified through a circular and shall take effect from the
date specified therein.

## 2. Programme Structure and Credits

2.1 The B.E. programme shall comprise eight semesters spread over four
academic years. Each academic year shall consist of two semesters of not less
than sixteen instructional weeks each.

2.2 A candidate shall be required to earn a minimum of 160 credits for the
award of the B.E. degree, distributed across basic sciences, engineering
sciences, professional core, professional electives, open electives, and
mandatory non-credit courses.

2.3 One credit shall correspond to one hour of lecture or tutorial per week,
or two hours of practical or laboratory work per week, per semester.

2.4 Professional elective courses shall be offered from the fifth semester
onwards. A candidate shall register for professional electives in accordance
with the pre-requisites notified by the Board of Studies of the department
concerned.

## 3. Registration

3.1 Every candidate shall register for the courses of a semester within the
dates notified by the Office of the Controller of Examinations. Late
registration shall attract a fee as prescribed.

3.2 A candidate shall not be permitted to register for a course unless the
pre-requisites specified for that course, if any, have been completed.

3.3 Registration for a semester shall be deemed incomplete unless all dues
payable to the College, including hostel and library dues, have been cleared.

## 4. Attendance Requirements

4.1 Attendance shall be reckoned from the date of commencement of instruction
of the semester as notified in the academic calendar, or from the date of
admission of the candidate, whichever is later. Attendance shall be computed
separately for each course, including laboratory courses.

4.2 A candidate shall be required to put in a minimum of seventy-five percent
(75%) of attendance in each course, computed on the total number of periods
of instruction held in that course during the semester, in order to be
eligible to appear for the semester end examination in that course. A
candidate who fails to secure the required seventy-five percent (75%)
attendance in any course shall be detained in that course and shall not be
permitted to appear for the semester end examination therein.

4.3 Attendance in laboratory courses shall be computed on the basis of the
number of laboratory sessions actually conducted. A laboratory session missed
shall not be made up except with the prior written permission of the Head of
the Department concerned and subject to availability of laboratory resources.

4.4 The attendance position of every candidate shall be displayed on the
College notice board and the student portal at the end of each month. Any
representation regarding the attendance so displayed shall be made within
seven working days of such display, after which the attendance recorded shall
be treated as final.

4.5 Condonation of shortage of attendance to the extent of ten percent (10%),
that is, for candidates securing attendance of not less than sixty-five
percent (65%) and less than seventy-five percent (75%) in a course, may be
granted by the Principal on the recommendation of the Head of the Department,
on genuine and verifiable medical grounds, and on payment of the condonation
fee prescribed by the College. Application for condonation shall be made in
the prescribed form together with supporting medical certification, not later
than ten days before the commencement of the semester end examinations.

4.6 Condonation under clause 4.5 shall not be granted more than twice during
the entire programme, and shall not be granted to a candidate whose
attendance in the course concerned is below sixty-five percent (65%) under
any circumstances whatsoever.

4.7 A candidate detained under clause 4.2 for want of attendance shall be
required to re-register for and repeat the course concerned in a subsequent
semester in which the course is offered.

## 5. Evaluation and Grading

5.1 The performance of a candidate in each course shall be evaluated for one
hundred (100) marks, comprising forty (40) marks for continuous internal
evaluation and sixty (60) marks for the semester end examination.

5.2 Continuous internal evaluation shall consist of two mid-semester
examinations of twenty (20) marks each, of which the better performance shall
be given a weightage of eighty percent (80%) and the other twenty percent
(20%).

5.3 A candidate shall be declared to have passed a course if the candidate
secures not less than forty percent (40%) of the marks in the semester end
examination and not less than forty percent (40%) of the marks in the
aggregate of internal evaluation and semester end examination taken together.

5.4 Grades shall be awarded on a ten-point scale. The Cumulative Grade Point
Average (CGPA) shall be computed as the weighted average of the grade points
secured in all courses registered, weighted by the credits assigned to each
course.

## 6. Promotion and Backlogs

6.1 A candidate shall be promoted from the second year to the third year only
if the candidate has secured not less than sixty percent (60%) of the total
credits offered up to the second year.

6.2 A backlog shall mean a course in which a candidate has been declared to
have failed, or in which the candidate has been detained for want of
attendance under clause 4.2.

6.3 A candidate shall clear all backlogs of the first and second years before
being permitted to register for the courses of the seventh semester.

6.4 The maximum period for completion of the programme shall be eight
academic years from the date of first admission.
